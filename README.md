An alien tourist's two-day review of planet Earth — a [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty entry by [@dizellacapital](https://make.fun/r/qxacafna).

## The review

> **Three stars. Lovely planet, bad logistics: gravity dropped my luggage twice, weather changed four times before lunch, and locals charge for water on a world that is 70% water. Sunsets are worth it.**

Three stars. Filed in earnest by a visitor who liked the planet and struggled with the logistics: gravity keeps dropping the luggage, the weather changes four times before lunch, and the locals charge for water on a world that is 70% water. The verdict stays warm — the sunsets are worth it.

## How it works

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes the review to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (fallback `/output`), validates it is a single line of 1–200 characters, and verifies the write by reading it back. The entry file is exactly 197 bytes, with no trailing newline.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat "$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt"
```
